About us

Your Neighbourhood Gathering Place

Ruths is a welcoming all-day spot in Malmö where restaurant meets bakery. We bake our own breads and pastries daily, serve breakfast through dinner, and offer a carefully curated selection of natural wines from small European producers. Whether you're stopping by for morning coffee, a leisurely lunch, or evening wine and dinner, you're welcome here just as you are.

Why choose us

  • In-House Bakery Fresh breads and pastries baked on-site every day, from sourdough loaves to cinnamon buns.
  • Natural Wine Selection Curated wines from small European producers, with changing by-the-glass options served on tap.
  • All-Day Service Open from 7 AM serving breakfast, lunch, and dinner, making us your go-to spot any time of day.
